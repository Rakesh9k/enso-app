# Enso Product Requirements

## Vision
An AI-powered wellness companion that helps people understand themselves through journaling, meditation, and pattern recognition.

## Core Features
1. **Journaling**: Text and voice journal entries
2. **Meditation**: Guided and unguided sessions
3. **AI Insights**: Daily, weekly, monthly reflections
4. **Pattern Engine**: Behavioral pattern detection
5. **Journey Road**: Visual progress tracking
6. **Rooms**: Emotional atmosphere environments
7. **Partner Program**: Referral and commission system

## Subscription Tiers
- **Free**: Basic journaling, meditation, limited insights
- **Premium**: Voice journaling, full AI insights, all rooms
- **Pro**: Advanced features (future)
