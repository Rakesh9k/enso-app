# Enso Theme System

## Design Philosophy
- Sanctuary > SaaS
- Paper + Ink + Breath
- Incomplete circle language
- Editorial calm

## Material Stack
- Paper (base world)
- Ink (meaning layer)
- Light (emotional temperature)
- Shadow (depth cue)
- Glass (frosted paper veil)

## Rooms
- Chalk, Paper, Mist, Dusk, Rain, Tide, Garden, Aurora

## Motion Tokens
- micro: 120ms
- tap: 180ms
- ui: 240ms
- reveal: 600ms
- bloom: 900ms
- breath: 3200ms
